1.1 Purpose 
The Enterprise Cognitive Assessment Architecture (ECAA) defines the engineering 
mechanisms through which Enterprise Cognitive Systems are systematically evaluated, 
measured and benchmarked. 
The architecture provides a standardized basis for assessing cognitive quality, 
organizational intelligence, governance effectiveness and engineering maturity. 
 

===== PAGE 2 =====
1.2 Design Philosophy 
Assessment shall evaluate enterprise cognition rather than infrastructure characteristics 
alone. 
Every assessment shall produce objective, explainable and evidence-based 
conclusions regarding the quality and maturity of Enterprise Cognitive Systems. 
 
1.3 Core Principle 
Every material cognitive capability shall be measurable through standardized evidence, 
repeatable assessment procedures and governed evaluation criteria. 
Assessment shall strengthen enterprise trust through transparency and continuous 
improvement.

2.1 Purpose 
The Enterprise Cognitive Quality Architecture (ECQA) defines the engineering 
mechanisms through which Enterprise Cognitive Systems establish, measure and 
improve cognitive quality. 
The architecture provides a structured quality model spanning reasoning, context, 
memory, knowledge, governance, learning and organizational intelligence.

characteristics alone. 
Every quality evaluation shall be evidence-based, explainable and continuously 
measurable. 
 
2.3 Core Principle

evaluation criteria and governed improvement objectives. 
Quality shall evolve together with enterprise cognition.
